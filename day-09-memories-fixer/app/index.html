Memories Fixer Everything happens on this device. Nothing is uploaded.

Put the dates back on your Snapchat memories

Snapchat's download loses when and where each memory was taken, and keeps captions in separate files. Drop the export here to fix both, then move the folder into Google Photos.

How to get your export

In Snapchat
Settings, then My Data
Tick
Memories, and "export your Memories"
Choose
JSON files, all time
When the email arrives
Download every ZIP it links to

Drop all the ZIPs in at once. The dates are in one of them (memories_history.json), and the photos and videos are spread across the rest.

Your files never leave this page. You can switch off Wi-Fi after it loads and it still works.
